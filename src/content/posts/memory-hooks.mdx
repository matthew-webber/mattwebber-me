## Reverse Incremental Search (`Ctrl+R` / `Ctrl+S`)

Once you're in the terminal's incremental history search, it's easy to forget which direction `Ctrl+R` and `Ctrl+S` take you.

- **`Ctrl+R`** = **Reverse** - takes you backward through your history (older commands)
- **`Ctrl+S`** = **Skipped** - takes you forward (back toward commands you already skipped past with `Ctrl+R`)
